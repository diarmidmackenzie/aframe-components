connecting-line2: world-unit extrusion, orthographic camera

Every stroke below is the same width — width:0.04, units:m — spread over a wide area, with the orthographic camera just 0.5 world units in front of the drawing plane.

Press Toggle and watch the strokes away from the centre.

  • BEFORE — the extrusion faces the camera position, so a stroke is narrowed by |d| / sqrt(d² + r²): right at the centre, a sixteenth of its width at the corners, and the axis-aligned rows vanish outright.
  • FIXED — under an orthographic projection the extrusion faces the view direction. Same width everywhere.

Antialiasing is off on purpose: that is what an offscreen PNG capture renders under, and what turns a thinned stroke into a missing one. Multisampling hides the effect entirely — which is why this looked like an export-only bug.

A three.js bug, fixed upstream by #34540 (r187); carried here per material until the bundled three has it. See also units-ortho.html.
